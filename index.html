Who we are

An independent R&D collective for hardware that has to actually work.

Vampires Technologies exists for the builds other studios pass on — the ones that need a circuit designed, a prototype machined, and firmware written by people who'll still be there when it needs debugging at 2am. We don't hand over concepts. We hand over hardware that's already been proven on our own bench, under our own name.

No templates, no outsourced guesswork, no roadmap borrowed from somewhere else. Just engineers who'd rather ship something real than talk about it.
